4 DOLLS · 1 LAST GIG

They cut us out of burlap,
sewed us shut, and threw us away.

We were nobody's keepers. Melvin traded his band for a contract and ended it on a rope. Rusty loved too hard and burned with the house. The Voice sold his name to the stage. The Pulse hammered until his heart gave out. Heaven didn't want us. Hell didn't dare.

So we crawled back through the stitch-holes, tuned our strings with grave-dust, and bought a warped old amplifier for one dollar. Everything after that was profit.

— you know your worth. Now sing it.

STRING-BENDER ✦ THE TRAITOR ✦ CONTRACT SIGNED ✦

string-bender · the traitor

MELVIN

“The Hanged”

Once a man. A gifted guitarist who traded his bandmates, his family and his friends for a contract, fame and money. He got everything — and died alone in an empty flat, hollowed out by his own success.

He finished the job with a rope. Heaven weighed his soul and found it too heavy. Hell screened him and threw the interview out — he'd only sow chaos there too. The noose isn't a costume. It's a receipt.

CASE CLOSED he begs for an encore he never gave
FOUR-STRING ✦ THE LOYALIST ✦ BURNED, NOT BURIED ✦

four-string · the loyalist

RUSTY

“The Ashed”

He was never a person. He's a hand-sewn rag doll — burlap skin, one button eye, a black-thread cross for the other. A boy called him his best friend and dreamed they'd grow up to be rock stars together.

The boy grew up. The box went to the attic. The house burned down. Everything turned to ash — except Rusty. Some fires make you softer. His made him louder.

HANDMADE still warm. still waiting by the mirror
VOCAL ✦ THE FACE ✦ SECOND NAME OF DOLLARS ✦

vocal · the face

THE VOICE

“Dollars' Second Name”

A voice that runs from a whisper to a scream without asking permission. The room goes quiet when he touches the mic. The room goes deaf when he lets go.

Every song is a confession. Every show is a rebirth. Fans know him from the very first note. Dollars isn't just the band. Dollars is his second name.

ON EVERY LIP first note. last bite.
DRUMS ✦ THE HEARTBEAT ✦ BEAT AGAINST BOREDOM ✦

drums · the heartbeat

THE PULSE

“The Heartbeat”

The band's pulse. His rhythm is the heartbeat every other song borrows. The kit is wired straight into his body — when he plays, you can't tell where the drum ends and he begins.

His hands are lightning on the cymbals. Every beat is a sentence against boredom. No drummer, no band. That's not an opinion — it's arithmetic.
