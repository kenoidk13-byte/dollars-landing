MELVIN

“The Hanged”

He was once a human being — a talented guitarist who played in underground bands. But his talent was poisoned by arrogance. For the sake of a contract, fame, and money, he betrayed his band and abandoned his family; he trampled on those who loved him and sold out his friends. He lived by the principle that only he mattered. He believed music was his ticket to immortality and that people were merely tools to achieve his ends.

“Rusty”

He was never a person. He's a hand-sewn rag doll — burlap skin, one button eye, a black-thread cross for the other. A boy called him his best friend and dreamed they'd grow up to be rock stars together.

The boy grew up. The box went to the attic. The house burned down. Everything turned to ash — except Rusty. Some fires make you softer. His made him louder.

drums · the heartbeat

STACY

“The Tempo”

She was neither a human nor a beloved childhood toy. Created as an anti-stress voodoo doll, she sat on the desk of a perpetually flustered Director in a drab, stuffy office. Whenever the Director faced looming deadlines, collapsing workflows, or simply didn't know what to do, he would stick pins into her.

She silently absorbed the folly, chaos, and hysterics of others. Her mouth was stitched shut with coarse thread so she could not speak. And she endured. Inside her canvas body beat a titanium metronome. She loved the System and the Rhythm, yet all around her was a shambles.
